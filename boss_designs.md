# Boss Designs for Pyzelda RPG

## Boss Encounters
1. **Fire Guardian** (Volcano Temple)
   - Phase 1: Basic fireballs, charges. Health: 50%.
   - Phase 2: Summons imps, lava eruption. Health: 50%-0%.
   - Weakness: Water magic in Phase 2.

2. **Water Serpent** (Underwater Ruins)
   - Phase 1: Tail whips, water jets. Health: 60%.
   - Phase 2: Floods arena, summons eels. Health: 60%-0%.
   - Weakness: Electric magic.

3. **Earth Titan** (Mountain Caves)
   - Phase 1: Rock throws, ground pounds. Health: 40%.
   - Phase 2: Creates pillars, earthquakes. Health: 40%-0%.
   - Weakness: Wind magic to destabilize.

4. **Air Phoenix** (Sky Islands)
   - Phase 1: Dive bombs, wind gusts. Health: 70%.
   - Phase 2: Rebirth from ashes, fire+wind combo. Health: 70%-0%.
   - Weakness: Earth magic to ground.

5. **Spirit Wraith** (Spirit Realm)
   - Phase 1: Possession, illusions. Health: 50%.
   - Phase 2: Soul drain, summons ghosts. Health: 50%-0%.
   - Weakness: Light magic.

6. **Shadow Lord** (Final Boss)
   - Phase 1: Dark orbs, shadow clones. Health: 60%.
   - Phase 2: Absorbs artifacts, elemental attacks. Health: 60%-30%.
   - Phase 3: Desperate mode, random chaos. Health: 30%-0%.
   - Weakness: All elements combined.
