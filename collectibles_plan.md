# Collectibles Plan

## Collectible Items
- **Heart Container**: Increases max health. Locations: (100,200) in forest_1, (500,300) in cave_1.
- **Rupee Pouch**: Increases gold capacity. Locations: (150,250) in meadow, (400,150) in ruins.
- **Magic Potion**: Restores magic. Locations: (200,100) in volcano, (300,400) in underwater.
- **Key**: Unlocks doors. Locations: (50,50) in bandit camp, (600,200) in tower.
- **Artifact Piece**: Advances story. Locations: Tied to main quests, e.g., Fire Artifact at (700,500) in volcano.
- **Rare Weapon**: Upgrades equipment. Locations: (800,100) in mountain, (250,600) in sky islands.
- **Lore Book**: Reveals story hints. Locations: (350,450) in spirit realm, (550,350) in haunted house.
