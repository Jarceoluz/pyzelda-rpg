# Enemy Designs for Pyzelda RPG

## New Enemy Types
1. **Flying Bat**: Flies around, swoops down to attack. Weakness: Fire magic. Behavior: Patrols air, dives at player.
2. **Slime Blob**: Splits when hit, regenerates. Weakness: Ice magic. Behavior: Bounces towards player, leaves trail.
3. **Stone Golem**: Slow but strong, throws rocks. Weakness: Water magic (erodes). Behavior: Charges, smashes ground.
4. **Wind Spirit**: Invisible until attacked, gusts push player. Weakness: Earth magic (grounds). Behavior: Teleports, wind blasts.
5. **Fire Imp**: Shoots fireballs, immune to fire. Weakness: Water magic. Behavior: Dances around, rapid fire.
6. **Ice Wraith**: Freezes player on contact. Weakness: Fire magic. Behavior: Floats, ice spikes.
7. **Poison Frog**: Jumps high, poisons on hit. Weakness: Healing magic. Behavior: Hops erratically.
8. **Shadow Assassin**: Stealthy, backstabs. Weakness: Light magic. Behavior: Hides in shadows, ambushes.
9. **Electric Eel**: Shocks in water, chains lightning. Weakness: Earth magic. Behavior: Swims fast, arcs electricity.
10. **Crystal Beetle**: Hard shell, reflects magic. Weakness: Blunt weapons. Behavior: Rolls into ball, charges.
11. **Vine Ent**: Roots player, summons vines. Weakness: Fire magic. Behavior: Stationary, vine whips.
12. **Sand Worm**: Bursts from ground, swallows. Weakness: Water magic. Behavior: Burrows, emerges suddenly.
13. **Ghost Pirate**: Phases through walls, curses. Weakness: Holy magic. Behavior: Teleports, spectral attacks.
14. **Lava Elemental**: Melts armor, burns. Weakness: Ice magic. Behavior: Lava pools, erupts.
15. **Thunder Bird**: Calls lightning, flies high. Weakness: Wind magic (blows away). Behavior: Circles, strikes from above.
16. **Mushroom Monster**: Spore clouds, hallucinates. Weakness: Fire magic. Behavior: Releases spores, confuses player.
