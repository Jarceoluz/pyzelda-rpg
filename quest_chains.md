# Quest Chains and Dependencies

## Main Quest Chain
- Collect Fire Artifact -> Collect Water Artifact -> Collect Earth Artifact -> Collect Air Artifact -> Collect Spirit Artifact -> Defeat Shadow Lord

## Side Quest Chains
- Meet Mentor -> Train with Mentor -> Craft Weapon
- Obtain Map from NPC -> Find Hidden Treasure
- Investigate Suspicious Activities -> Uncover Conspiracy -> Alliance with Faction
- Fetch Lost Ring -> Deliver Message (unlocks info)
- Collect Herbs -> Heal the Sick
- Explore Ruins -> Uncover Lore Clues -> Aid in Main Quest

## Prerequisites
- Time-sensitive quests like Escort Merchant have no chains but must be started promptly.
- Combat quests like Defeat Goblins can be done anytime but may be suggested by NPCs.
- Exploration quests like Puzzle Tower unlock rewards that aid other quests.
