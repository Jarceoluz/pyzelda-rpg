# Quest Ideas for Pyzelda RPG

## Main Quests
1. **Collect Fire Artifact**: Find and retrieve the Fire Artifact from the Volcano Temple. Objectives: Navigate lava puzzles, defeat fire guardians. Rewards: Fire magic upgrade. Prerequisites: None.
2. **Collect Water Artifact**: Retrieve Water Artifact from Underwater Ruins. Objectives: Solve water-based puzzles, fight aquatic enemies. Rewards: Water magic upgrade. Prerequisites: Fire Artifact collected.
3. **Collect Earth Artifact**: Obtain Earth Artifact from Mountain Caves. Objectives: Climb cliffs, battle earth golems. Rewards: Earth magic upgrade. Prerequisites: Water Artifact collected.
4. **Collect Air Artifact**: Secure Air Artifact from Sky Islands. Objectives: Glide between islands, defeat wind spirits. Rewards: Air magic upgrade. Prerequisites: Earth Artifact collected.
5. **Collect Spirit Artifact**: Claim Spirit Artifact from Spirit Realm. Objectives: Navigate ethereal planes, confront spirit bosses. Rewards: Spirit magic upgrade. Prerequisites: All other artifacts collected.

## Side Quests
6. **Defeat Goblins**: Kill 10 goblins in the forest. Objectives: Hunt goblins. Rewards: Health potion. Prerequisites: None.
7. **Fetch Lost Ring**: Retrieve a ring from a bandit camp. Objectives: Infiltrate camp, collect ring. Rewards: Accessory item. Prerequisites: None.
8. **Escort Merchant**: Protect merchant to town. Objectives: Escort safely, defend from attacks. Rewards: Gold. Prerequisites: None. Time-sensitive: 5 minutes.
9. **Collect Herbs**: Gather 5 rare herbs in the meadow. Objectives: Find and pick herbs. Rewards: Healing items. Prerequisites: None.
10. **Rescue Villager**: Save villager from monster lair. Objectives: Enter lair, defeat monster, rescue. Rewards: Gratitude, item. Prerequisites: None.
11. **Puzzle Tower**: Solve puzzles in ancient tower. Objectives: Complete 3 floors of puzzles. Rewards: Treasure chest. Prerequisites: None.
12. **Hunt Wolves**: Kill 5 wolves in the wilds. Objectives: Track and defeat wolves. Rewards: Wolf pelt. Prerequisites: None.
13. **Deliver Message**: Carry message to distant village. Objectives: Travel to village, deliver. Rewards: Information for main quest. Prerequisites: None.
14. **Defeat Bandits**: Clear bandit hideout. Objectives: Fight bandits, loot hideout. Rewards: Gold and weapons. Prerequisites: None.
15. **Find Hidden Treasure**: Locate buried treasure using map. Objectives: Follow clues, dig up treasure. Rewards: Rare items. Prerequisites: Obtain map from NPC.
16. **Train with Mentor**: Complete training challenges. Objectives: Pass 3 skill tests. Rewards: Stat boost. Prerequisites: Meet mentor.
17. **Explore Ruins**: Investigate ancient ruins. Objectives: Explore, find artifacts. Rewards: Lore clues. Prerequisites: None.
18. **Protect Farm**: Defend farm from raiders. Objectives: Repel attackers. Rewards: Food supplies. Prerequisites: None.
19. **Craft Weapon**: Gather materials for weapon. Objectives: Collect iron, wood. Rewards: Custom sword. Prerequisites: Blacksmith quest.
20. **Uncover Conspiracy**: Investigate suspicious activities. Objectives: Gather evidence, confront culprit. Rewards: Alliance with faction. Prerequisites: Story progress.
21. **Heal the Sick**: Find cure for village illness. Objectives: Collect ingredients. Rewards: Villager thanks. Prerequisites: None.
22. **Race Challenge**: Win a race against locals. Objectives: Complete course fastest. Rewards: Trophy. Prerequisites: None.
23. **Ghost Hunt**: Banish ghosts from haunted house. Objectives: Solve mystery, defeat ghosts. Rewards: Spirit item. Prerequisites: None.
24. **Fishing Contest**: Catch rare fish. Objectives: Fish in lake. Rewards: Fish dish. Prerequisites: None.
25. **Alchemy Experiment**: Create potion. Objectives: Mix ingredients. Rewards: Potion. Prerequisites: Learn recipe.
